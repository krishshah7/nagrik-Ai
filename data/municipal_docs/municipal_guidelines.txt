Reporting Guidelines for Citizens (Sample Document)

To report illegal construction, citizens should submit a complaint with the exact location, photographs if possible, and a clear description of the unauthorized structure. The Town Planning department investigates such complaints and can issue a stop-work notice within 15 days if the construction is found to be unauthorized.

Every complaint filed on Nagrik AI is assigned a status: Pending, In Progress, or Resolved, and can be tracked from the citizen's dashboard at any time.

For urgent, life-threatening civic issues, such as open manholes or collapsed structures, citizens should also call the municipal helpline directly in addition to filing an online complaint, since those situations need immediate attention.

Complaints can be filed in any of the city's official wards. If a citizen is unsure which ward they belong to, the nearest ward office can confirm this over phone or in person.
