Municipal Schemes and Departmental Responsibilities (Sample Document)

Drainage and sewage complaints are the responsibility of the Drainage and Sewage department, which operates under the Public Health Engineering wing of the municipal corporation. Waterlogging complaints during the monsoon season are treated as high priority and must be attended to within 12 hours.

The AMRUT scheme (Atal Mission for Rejuvenation and Urban Transformation) funds improvements to drainage, water supply, and sewerage infrastructure in participating cities.

Water supply complaints, including pipeline leakage, contamination, and irregular supply timings, are handled by the Water Works department.

The Swachh Bharat Mission (Urban) funds solid waste management infrastructure, including door-to-door garbage collection and public toilet construction.
